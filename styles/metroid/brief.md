# Metroid Vibes

Atmospheric exploration music in the spirit of late-80s / 90s sci-fi adventure
games: lonely caverns, alien ruins, distant machinery. Dark modes (phrygian,
aeolian, harmonic minor), chromatic-mediant moves (i -> biii, i -> bII), long
pedal tones. Ostinato bass and pulse arpeggios carry the motion; the lead is
sparse, lonely and memorable, with lots of space. Drums are minimal and
mechanical — often absent — and only push harder in "pursuit" sections.

Tempo 70-105 BPM, straight time, almost no humanization (sequenced feel).
Instrument choices should suit OPL3 FM: square/saw leads, crystal and
atmosphere pads, synth bass.
