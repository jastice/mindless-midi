# Metroid Vibes

Atmospheric exploration music in the spirit of the 8- and 16-bit Metroid games:
lonely caverns, alien ruins, distant machinery. Dark modes (phrygian, aeolian,
harmonic minor), chromatic-mediant moves (i -> biii, i -> bII), long pedal
tones. Ostinato bass and pulse arpeggios carry the motion; the lead is sparse,
lonely and memorable, with lots of space. Drums are minimal and mechanical,
often absent, and only push harder in "pursuit" sections.

Besides a few untagged, general forms, write one area form (with its own
palette, tempo, keys and instrument swaps, and material tagged with that
palette) for each of these Super Metroid-style areas:

- Crateria surface: rain on a dead planet, slow (60-76 BPM), ominous choir pad,
  timpani-like low toms, a distant heroic horn call.
- Brinstar jungle: overgrown and alive, 100-118 BPM, syncopated bass, busy hand
  percussion, bright mallet arpeggios, a playful ocarina melody, dorian.
- Norfair lava: driving and hot, 120-140 BPM, harmonic minor / phrygian
  dominant, relentless bass ostinato with the flat second, synth brass.
- Maridia depths: underwater, 80-96 BPM, a laid-back funky fretless bass groove,
  bubbling vibraphone, dorian, airy pads.
- Wrecked ship: haunted, 66-84 BPM, locrian / tritones, ghostly choir, a music
  box, creaking percussion, almost no rhythm.

Write original material evoking these moods; never quote the games' melodies.
Instrument choices should suit OPL3 FM: square/saw leads, crystal and
atmosphere pads, synth bass.
