# 70s Funk

James Brown, The Meters and Tower of Power: a tight 16th-note pocket. The
drums play the one hard, with ghost-note snares, 16th hi-hats and an open hat
on the "a" of four. The finger bass is syncopated, with octave pops and
chromatic approaches. Chicken-scratch muted guitar plays single-note 16th
riffs, the clavinet plays percussive shell voicings, and a horn section stabs
on the one, with a tenor sax lead and trumpet answers.

Harmony is one- and two-chord vamps: i7, i9 - IV9 dorian, i11 - bVII9 - IV9,
a minor blues with V7#9, and a bVImaj7 - bVII7 bridge. Tempo 96-112, slight
16th swing (~0.1), tight humanization. Areas:

- On the one: JB dominant vamps in mixolydian (I9, I7#9 - IV9), slap bass,
  104-118.
- Mothership: P-Funk, with a Minimoog lead and bass, string synth, claps on 2
  and 4, and a slinky 96-106.
- Uptown soul: Stevie / Philly at 84-96, with ionian maj7 - m9 - V13
  progressions, Rhodes, strings, harmonica lead and a Jamerson-style bass.
