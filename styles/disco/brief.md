# Mirrorball Disco

Late-seventies dance floor, in the spirit of Chic, Earth Wind & Fire, the Bee
Gees and Donna Summer. Four on the floor, open hi-hats on every offbeat, claps
on two and four; a bass that hops octaves in eighths; chicken-scratch 16th
guitar; electric-piano chops on the offbeats; a string section that soars in
long notes and tumbles down in 16th runs; brass stabs answering it.

Harmony: minor-seventh vamps (i7 - iv7, dorian i7 - IV7), the full circle of
fifths (i7 - iv7 - bVII7 - bIIImaj7 - bVImaj7 - iiø7 - V7), bVImaj7 - bVII - i,
sus-chord dominants. Tempo 116-126, the faintest 16th swing. Areas:

- Philly strings: 104-114, major, sweet maj7 soul with a vibraphone and flute.
- Space disco: 120-128, minor triads, a 16th-note sequencer bass, an
  arpeggiator, saw lead, vocoder choir, drum machine and syn-toms.
- Roller rink: 108-118, major and mixolydian, a rink organ and handclaps on
  every beat.

Write original material evoking the mood; never quote any song's melodies.
