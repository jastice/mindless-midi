# Synthwave

80s-inspired outrun and retrowave: night drives on neon-lit highways. Pulsing
octave or driving 16th-note synth bass, relentless 16th-note saw arpeggios,
wide analog pads, gated chord stabs, a soaring saw lead with long notes, and a
drum machine with big gated snares, steady 8th hats and tom sweeps. Minor
progressions like i-bVI-bIII-bVII and i-bVII-bVI-bVII, plus a slow, warm
"sunset" area (major/lydian, 84-94 BPM) and a fast "neon chase" area.

Tempo 96-108 BPM, straight, almost no humanization.
