# Electro Swing

1930s swing and gypsy jazz over a modern four-on-the-floor club beat: a
swinging clarinet lead, muted trumpet answers, brass section stabs, Manouche
"la pompe" rhythm guitar, a honky-tonk piano, upright bass alternating two-beat
and walking, swung hi-hats with open hats on the offbeats and claps on 2 and 4.
Minor-key gypsy-jazz harmony (i6 - V7, iv6, bVI7 - V7, harmonic minor runs),
plus a major-key Charleston/ragtime "ballroom" area.

Tempo 118-128 BPM, 8th-note swing around 0.6.
