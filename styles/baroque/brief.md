# Baroque Court

An early-eighteenth-century chamber concerto, in the spirit of Vivaldi,
Corelli, Handel and Bach. A solo violin and an oboe trade figures over a basso
continuo: a cello in walking or repeated eighths, a theorbo realising the
chords, a harpsichord in 16th-note broken chords, and a string ripieno for the
tuttis. Melodies are built from short rhetorical figures (figura corta,
suspirans, scale runs, string crossings) that sequence from chord to chord.

Harmony: minor keys with a major dominant; half-bar circle-of-fifths
sequences, the descending-tetrachord lament, an old ground bass, the
Neapolitan before a cadence, diminished sevenths on the leading tone. Tempo
92-112, straight. Long lead notes take trills from the upper neighbour; pieces
slow into the final chord, which is major (a Picardy third). Areas:

- Slow air: 60-72, major, a flute singing long lines over a walking bass.
- Royal fireworks: 104-120, major, a natural trumpet with fanfare figures,
  kettledrums on tonic and dominant.
- Organ loft: 80-96, minor, a toccata and fugato for organ alone: a subject,
  its answer, the full organ over long pedal notes.
