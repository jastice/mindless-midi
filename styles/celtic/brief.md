# Celtic Tavern

An Irish session by the fire: jigs in 6/8. A tin whistle carries the tune with
cuts and rolls (grace notes), a fiddle doubles and answers, bouzouki strums
open fifths (power voicings), a harp arpeggiates in triplets, an accordion
holds chords, a double bass plays boom-chick, and a bodhrán drives the
"DUM-da-da DUM-da-da" with top-end tones on the high tom.

Written as 2 beats per bar (dotted quarters) on a triplet grid: eighths are
1/3 beat, drum patterns use 3 or 6 steps per beat. Tunes are AABB, 8 bars per
part. Modal harmony: i - bVII dorian/aeolian, i - IV dorian, bIII - bVII. Tempo
104-120 dotted-quarter BPM, straight, some humanization. Areas:

- Fair day: mixolydian and major jigs (I - bVII, I - IV - V) with a tenor
  banjo lead.
- Slow air: a lament at 40-50, with a low whistle, a misty choir, harp and a
  tonic drone.
- Highland march: a 6/8 pipe march in A mixolydian, with highland pipes over
  drones and a pipe-band snare.
