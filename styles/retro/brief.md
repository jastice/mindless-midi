# Retro Games

Upbeat 8/16-bit video game music: overworld adventure themes, cheerful town
loops, castle stages and victory fanfares. Catchy, singable hooks in major,
mixolydian, dorian and heroic minor (i - bVI - bVII). Driving bass with octave
bounces, fast broken-chord arpeggios, a counter-melody in thirds/sixths, and a
tight, punchy kit.

Tempo 110-160 BPM, straight 16ths (occasionally light shuffle), sequenced feel.
Square/saw/pulse leads, synth bass, simple drums.
