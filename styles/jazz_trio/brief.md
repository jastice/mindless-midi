# Jazz Piano Trio

A relaxed piano trio in a late-night club: piano (rootless left-hand voicings
plus right-hand melody), upright bass walking quarter notes with chromatic
approach tones, and drums keeping a swung ride pattern with hi-hat on 2 and 4.
Standard-style harmony: ii-V-I in major and minor, I-vi-ii-V turnarounds,
iii-VI-ii-V, 12-bar blues, backdoor bVII7. Melodies are bebop-flavoured eighth
lines mixed with lyrical phrases.

Tempo 96-170 BPM, 8th-note swing ~0.75, generous humanization.
