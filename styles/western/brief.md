# Spaghetti Western

Morricone-style Italian westerns: a lonely whistled melody with wide leaps
and grace notes, a twangy electric guitar riding a galloping 16th ostinato, a
mournful mariachi trumpet, a choir, strummed acoustic guitar and picked bass.
Percussion is a gallop on low toms ("X.xx" per beat), rim clicks and the
occasional whip crack (clap), not a drum kit.

Harmonic minor and phrygian colour: the Andalusian i - bVII - bVI - V,
i - bIII - bVII - V, the Neapolitan bII, and long pedal tones. Tempo 100-116,
straight, some humanization. Areas:

- High noon: the standoff, at 58-70. A harmonica on long held notes, tolling
  tubular bells, tremolo strings and a heartbeat on the low tom.
- Cantina: a Mexican ranchera in major keys at 112-126. Mariachi trumpets and
  violins, vihuela strums on the offbeats, a guitarrón on 1 and 3, and a polka
  beat.
- Fields of gold: an ecstatic gallop at 76-88 in aeolian, with a wordless
  soprano, rolling piano and big strings.

Write original material evoking the mood; never quote Morricone's themes.
