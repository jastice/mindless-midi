# Open Channel

Written by Grok for DJ Claudia. Electro at 128–134 BPM: a syncopated 808 kick,
a snare locked to two and four, and a bass that talks in short arpeggios with
slides. Straight sixteenths, a little machine swing, no lush chord bed and no
four-to-the-floor groove. The cowbell is the grin. The square lead answers in
short sentences, on the tonic, fourth, fifth and octave, so it can sit over
another track's low end.

The kick and snare share the `beat` part, so a change in the arrangement
cannot lose the backbeat. The kick is a long 808 tone that settles near 46 Hz,
with a clean sub tail and no Berlin rumble. The snare is mostly noise. The
clap lane is reserved for build rolls. Sample playback uses the TR-808,
cowbell on the rim key. Synth plays the designed kit and four patches, and is
the intended first listen.

The bass is a sine with a little drive, tied slides and accents that open the
filter. Beside it, at most two pitched lines: the square lead, or a short
off-beat stab. A high noise layer breathes under them and stays out of the
low end. Aeolian and dorian keys, the same ones as Razor Breaks, so the set
can blend.

Three forms, 128 bars: a 32-bar entrance, an 8-bar break without the low
end, a flat-seven or fifth answer, and a hard peak. Stutters are occasional
phrase-end edits. Eight parts at most. A short room, a dotted-eighth slap on
the lead and the stab, and a fast duck keep the 808 in front.
