# Berlin Techno

Written by hand, not by `//tools/generate`. For DJ Claudia's pack only.

Darker, heavier and more spacious than minimal techno. 130 to 138 bpm, 4/4,
straight sixteenths. Minor and phrygian keys.

The low end is the kick and its rumble (the kit's `rumble` and `sub`), so the
bass is a held root or a few off-beat thuds, never a line. An off-beat open
hat, a sparse clap, a ride that arrives at the peak, low tuned toms, a
metallic tick. One dark stab with a long tail, a single struck metal tone, a
bell on a loop of three or five bars, a drone that barely moves, a noise wash
that climbs through each phrase.

One chord, held for eight bars. Sections of 16 and 32 bars: a breakdown
without the kick, a build, a peak without the drone. At most eight parts at
once, two pitched lines, two noise layers. Each part is tiny; the interest is
in what enters and leaves.

Sounds are tuned by measurement (`src/sound/berlin_techno.test.ts`).
