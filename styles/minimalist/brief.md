# Minimalist Piano

Calm, repetitive and slowly evolving, after Philip Glass, Steve Reich, Erik
Satie and Ludovico Einaudi: rolling broken-chord piano arpeggios (including
3-against-2 triplets), a marimba-like interlocking counter pattern, sustained
soft strings, and a sparse, singing upper melody. Simple diatonic and modal
progressions that shift slowly (often one chord per bar or two). No drums.

Tempo 60-120 BPM, straight, light humanization.
