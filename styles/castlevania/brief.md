# Castlevania Vibes

16-bit gothic action in the spirit of the classic Castlevania games: a heroic
minor-key lead on overdriven guitar, harpsichord 16th arpeggios (Alberti and
Bach-prelude figures, toccata pedal-tone lines), a violin countermelody,
palm-muted power chords, a pipe organ pad, a driving octave or gallop bass
and a rock kit. Baroque harmony in harmonic minor: circle-of-fifths sequences
(i - iv - bVII - bIII - bVI - iiø7 - V7 - i), the Andalusian cadence, the
Neapolitan bII and i - bVI - bVII - i. Tempo 132-156, straight, tight.

Areas:

- Clock tower: 140-156, a harpsichord lead in ticking 16th sequences, a music
  box and rim-click clockwork.
- Catacombs: 84-100, phrygian, a haunted flute, funeral bells, a choir and
  slow footsteps on the low tom.
- Chapel: 96-112, an organ fugue and chorale with organ pedals, a choir and
  no drums.

Write original material evoking the mood; never quote the games' melodies.
