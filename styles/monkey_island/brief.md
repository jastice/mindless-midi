# Monkey Island Vibes

Sunny Caribbean adventure-game music from the golden age of point-and-click
(late 80s / early 90s, AdLib era): calypso and reggae-tinged grooves, steel
drums and marimba, a wooden flute, island guitar skanking on the offbeats, a
bouncy root-fifth bass and hand percussion. Mostly cheerful and a bit
mischievous (major and mixolydian, I-IV-V7 and ii-V turnarounds), with a
slower tavern feel and one spooky "ghost ship" area: harmonic minor, a creaky
organ and a spectral clarinet, skeleton-jig percussion.

Tempo 100-126 BPM, light 16th swing, some humanization. Write original
material evoking the mood; never quote the games' melodies.
