# Low Log

An original Grok composition: a slow pocket where the bass line is the song.
Straight **4/4**, about **108–118 BPM**, light human touch, no swing. The log
is a short, syncopated bass cell, a little late, and hummable on its own.
Chords arrive about a half-beat after the bar. Kick and shaker stay sparse.
No four-on-the-floor hats, no busy lead.

The lead is absent for whole sections. When it enters, it answers in the
gaps the bass left, with short notes, never a soaring line. Dorian for the
quiet scenes; the answer may sit in mixolydian so its reply is a touch
brighter. No new mode, no odd bar.

Three densities, harmonies bound to their labels:

- **Bare log**: bass and a rare kick. The cell is the piece.
- **Soft pocket**: the same cell, with a pad that lands late.
- **The answer**: a slightly busier log, and a quiet electric piano in the holes.

A small ritardando into the final chord. Production: a dry centered bass, a
small dark room on the pad and the piano, almost nothing on the drums. The
pad may dip under the kick. The bass does not.

All themes and figures are original; no quotations. This is a checked-in corpus,
so the normal build needs no generation API.
