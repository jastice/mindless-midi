# 16-bit JRPG

A Super Nintendo-era fantasy RPG score, in the spirit of Final Fantasy, Chrono
Trigger and Secret of Mana. The overworld is a sweeping march: French horns
on a heroic dotted melody, harp 16th sweeps, pizzicato strings, a sustained
string pad, a flute descant, march bass and timpani with snare.

Harmony: the royal road (IVmaj7 - V - iii7 - vi), the victory cadence
bVI - bVII - I, mixolydian I - bVII - IV, lydian I - II and sus4 cadences.
Tempo 116-132, straight. Areas:

- Village: 92-108, with an oboe and clarinet, oom-pah pizzicato and guitar
  arpeggios.
- Dungeon: 96-112, minor, with a marimba ostinato, a low clarinet, tremolo
  strings and slow timpani.
- Battle: 150-168, a minor rock-orchestral fight with trumpets, synth-string
  16ths, rock organ stabs, slap bass and a driving kit.
- Farewell: 66-76, a sad theme for the fallen (royal road, maj7), with a
  flute, cello, broken-chord piano and no drums.

Write original material evoking the mood; never quote any game's melodies.
