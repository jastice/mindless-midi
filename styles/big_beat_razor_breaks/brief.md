# Razor Breaks

Written by Astra (GPT-6) for DJ Claudia. Big beat at 126–134 BPM: broken drums, a
distorted bass hook, clipped resonant teeth and clangorous FM power stabs.
The rhythm is the lead. No four-to-the-floor grooves, walking bass, lush
chord bed or continuous solo. Small sixteenth swing and quiet snare ghosts
give the machine some shoulders.

The kick and snare share the anchored `break` part so arrangement changes
cannot lose the backbeat. A short kick settles at 53 Hz; the snare has a
190 Hz body and a broad wire crack. Dark hats, dry metal rim and tuned toms
fill the spaces. The clap lane is reserved for build rolls. Sample playback
uses pinned 909, 808 and LM-2 recordings; Synth plays the designed kit and
five patches. Synth is the intended first listen.

The bass has a mono sub oscillator under a driven saw, staccato answers and
occasional tied octave slides. Two pitched lines at most: the low teeth
riff, a high siren answer, or a steel stab. The gated noise texture is sparse
and high-passed, not a sustained harmonic cushion. Power chords and
aeolian/dorian keys let the set blend into Claudia's existing styles.

Three forms, 104–128 bars: a 16-bar DJ entrance, riffs that change every few
bars, eight-bar breaks, mostly four-bar builds and a hard return. Stutters
are occasional phrase-end edits; fills include a final beat of silence.
Eight parts at most. A short room, restrained delay, fast duck release and
parallel drum compression preserve the attacks and the holes between them.

Validation covers the corpus, audible breakbeat onsets, deterministic
arrangement, transitions with the other styles, and finite synth output.
First auditioned in Claudia on 2026-11-17. The drum level is matched for
the handover from her softer styles without flattening the break's attacks.
