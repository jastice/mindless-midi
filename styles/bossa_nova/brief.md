# Bossa Nova

Rio de Janeiro, around 1960, after Jobim, João Gilberto and Getz: a nylon-string
guitar playing the syncopated batida, an upright bass on root and fifth with
anticipations, brushed hi-hat eighths, a cross-stick bossa clave and a soft
kick on the surdo rhythm. A breathy tenor sax melody with long held notes,
repeated notes and anticipations, flute answers, sparse piano fills and a
soft Ogerman-style string pad.

Written in Brazilian 2/4 notation: one 4-beat bar holds two bars of cut time,
so the two-bar 3-2 / 2-3 bossa clave (x..x..x...x..x.. on a 16th grid) fits
in one drum pattern. Tempo 62-74 here means 124-148 in cut time; 16th-note
swing around 0.1, gentle humanization.

Jobim-style harmony in major: Imaj7 - II7 - ii7 - bII7, IVmaj7 - iv6 - iii7 -
VI7b9 - ii7 - V7b9, diminished passing chords, I6/9 - II7#11, backdoor bVII9.
Areas:

- Samba-jazz club: 84-98 (168-196 cut time), dorian/minor ii-V vamps, a
  trumpet lead, partido-alto piano, vibraphone, samba ride and ganzá.
- Saudade at dusk: 50-60, aeolian ballads with iiø7 - V7b9, an alto flute,
  cello lines, string section and brush taps.
