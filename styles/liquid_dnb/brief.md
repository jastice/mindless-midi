# Liquid Drum & Bass

LTJ Bukem, Calibre and Hospital Records at 170-176 BPM. Two-step and rolling
breakbeats (kick on 1 and the "and" of 3, snare on 2 and 4, ghost snares,
shuffling 16th hats). A deep sub bass holds long notes under warm Rhodes
voicings (m9, maj9, add9), lush pads, a jazzy flute lead, vocal "oohs" and
vibraphone or bell arpeggios. Long sections: 16-bar intros, 32-bar drops and
breakdowns.

Harmony: slow-moving minor chords, two bars each: im9 - bVImaj9 - bIIImaj7 -
bVII6, im9 - iv9, dorian im9 - IV9, iv9 - v7 - bVImaj7 - bVII6. Straight, with
almost no humanization. Areas:

- Sunrise: a euphoric major/lydian roller (IVmaj9 - iii7 - vi9 - Vadd9) with
  bell plucks.
- Jungle: old-school 164-170 with chopped amen breaks, a Reese bass, rave
  stabs and dub chords over one-chord minor pressure.
