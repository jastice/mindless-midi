# Midnight Platform

An original Astra composition: the last train, rain on glass, a small jazz club
somewhere beyond the station. A **5/4 jazz noir quintet, grouped 3 + 2**: warm
vibraphone lead, tenor sax replies, acoustic piano, upright bass and soft kit.
92–126 BPM depending on the scene; moderate eighth-note swing and human touch.

The bass and ride explain the five without counting it aloud: a longer three-beat
stride followed by two beats home. Piano shells and rootless voicings leave room
for bass. Melodies have a clear question, a clipped pickup and a longer answer on
the second group. Tenor features contrast with the vibraphone head; they should
trade phrases instead of soloing at full density together.

Three scenes, with harmonies bound to their section labels:

- **Last train**: Dorian i9–IV9 vamp, with a relative-major detour for the sax.
- **Rain on the platform**: slower minor iiø7–V7b9 movement; let the drums drop
  away in the bridge and leave a piano-and-bass goodbye.
- **First service**: bright major turnarounds; a borrowed iv6 briefly shadows
  daybreak before the head returns.

Use five- and ten-beat motifs. Chromatic bass approaches belong on the final beat
before a chord change; sustained two-note bass patterns give the walk a rest.
Fills also occupy exactly five beats. Restrained ritardando into the final chord.
Production: a small dark club, little reverb on bass or drums, no dance pumping,
no long echo, with vibraphone and tenor gently apart in stereo.

All themes and figures are original; no quotations. This is a checked-in corpus,
so the normal build needs no generation API.
