# Café Musette

A Parisian bal-musette in 3/4. A swirling accordion valse in harmonic minor
with eighth-note runs, arpeggios, augmented seconds and ornaments. Gypsy-jazz
guitar plays the pompe on beats two and three, an upright bass the "oom" on
one (root, then fifth), a clarinet sighs in answer, a period banjo runs
arpeggios, a bandoneon breathes chords and brushes keep a soft waltz.

Harmony: i - iv - V7 - i, the I7 that tips into iv, the relative major
(bVII7 - bIII), viio7 passing chords, iv6 - V7 - i6 gypsy turnarounds and
bVI7 - V7. Tempo 150-184, 8th swing ~0.35, lively humanization. Areas:

- Java: the cheeky, swaying major-key java at 126-144.
- Rainy Montmartre: a cinematic valse lente at 96-116, with broken-chord
  piano, accordion, glockenspiel and strings, in aeolian.

No drum kit: no hi-hats or cymbals.
