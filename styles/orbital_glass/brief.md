# Orbital Glass

An original Astra composition: a small observatory turning above a blue planet.
Weightless chamber electronica in **7/4, grouped 2 + 2 + 3**, 72–90 BPM. Celesta,
vibraphone, distant crystal replies, warm synth pad and a soft bass foundation;
no drums. Leave air between notes. The melody should be something one can hum.

The signature is a **three-beat vibraphone cell against seven-beat bars**. It
continues across barlines and meets the downbeat again every three bars. Mix it
with seven-beat figures for moments of alignment. Let slow major sevenths,
added ninths and suspended chords carry the motion, rather than a busy lead.

Three scenes have their own harmonic material and candidate keys:

- **Sunlit rings**: Lydian colour, celesta, luminous Imaj7–IIadd9 motion.
- **Umbra**: a music-box beacon, Dorian minor, fewer parts and longer shadows.
- **Earthrise**: major-key warmth, a rising call, an opening-out into full ensemble.

Bind progressions to sections so each scene retains its arc. Repeated A sections
bring back the same call; the counter answers through its gaps. A short breath at
the end, with a modest ritardando. Production: long dark room, restrained dotted
quarter echoes on bells, a wide pad, and a dry centered bass.

All themes and figures are original; no quotations. This is a checked-in corpus,
so the normal build needs no generation API.
