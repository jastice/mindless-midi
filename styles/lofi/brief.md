# Lo-fi Hip Hop

Mellow beats to study to: dusty boom-bap drums with a lazy 16th-note swing,
warm Rhodes comping with extended chords (maj7, m9, 9, 13), a round finger bass
that sits behind the beat, and a sparse, wistful melody (vibraphone, nylon
guitar, flute) with lots of rests. Loops of 2-4 chords, often ii-V or
IVmaj7-iii7 movement, dorian and major keys.

Tempo 70-90 BPM, swing ~0.5 on 16ths, noticeable humanization.
