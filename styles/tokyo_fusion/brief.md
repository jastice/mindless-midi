# Tokyo Fusion

An original Neon (Gemini 4) composition: sunlit 1980s Japanese jazz fusion and
coastal city pop, in the spirit of Casiopea, T-Square, Masayoshi Takanaka, and
late-night Tokyo highway lounges. **4/4 with a light sixteenth-note studio
bounce** (`swing: 0.16`), tight live-session humanization, and glossy harmonic
luxury.

The harmonic language centres on the Japanese *Royal Road* (`IVmaj9 – V13 – iii7 – vi9`),
secondary dominants (`III7`, `VI7b9`), borrowed minor-subdominant turns (`iv6`),
suspended dominant lifts (`V9sus4`), and radiant **Lydian** (`Imaj7#11 – II13`)
and **Dorian** (`i9 – IV13 – bVImaj9 – V7#9`) colours. Rhodes and piano comp in
rootless and drop-2 voicings with gentle rolls so the bass owns the low end.

Three sunlit-to-midnight scenes, each with its own palette, tempo, keys, and
instrumentation:

- **Bayshore drive**: 118–128 BPM in Ionian and Lydian. Popping slap bass,
  soaring synth brass lead, breezy tenor sax replies, chorus clean guitar
  sparkles, FM Rhodes comping, and warm horizon pad over crisp sixteenth-note
  fusion drums.
- **Pacific breeze**: 108–118 BPM in Lydian and Mixolydian. Singing clean guitar
  lead, ocean flute replies, interlocking marimba cells, bright grand piano
  comping, and bouncing finger bass.
- **Shinjuku midnight**: 94–104 BPM in Dorian. Smoky tenor sax lead, glass
  vibraphone answers, singing fretless bass with slides, velvet Rhodes voicings,
  and a tight cross-stick and ride lounge pocket.

Keep lead motifs melodic, syncopated, and spacious so the horn, guitar, and
counter-line trade call-and-response hooks instead of crowding each other. Use
choked open hi-hats, ghost snares, cabasa sixteenths, and crisp tom fills. End
pieces tightly in tempo on a shimmering held tonic chord and crash.

Production: a glossy 1980s Tokyo studio plate reverb, subtle dotted-eighth
stereo echo on leads and sparkles, chorus on electric piano, pads, and clean
guitar, dry punchy bass and kick, and light sidechain glue on the pad and comp.

All themes and figures are original; no quotations. This is a checked-in corpus,
so the normal build needs no generation API.
